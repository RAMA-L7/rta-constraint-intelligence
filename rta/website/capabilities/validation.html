Capability · Validation

Findings you can trust, provenance you can follow

A deterministic rule engine checks every constraint and reports findings with a stable rule code, severity, evidence and the exact source line.

The problem

SDC errors hide until signoff

Delays that exceed their clock period, duplicate clock definitions, unresolved references, contradictory windows — these surface late, cost iteration cycles, and are hard to trace in large constraint files.

How it works

Every finding is structured

Findings carry a stable identity derived from the constraint itself — never from fragile message text — so they can be diffed across runs and baselines. Each finding records the rule, severity, affected object, evidence and source line.
