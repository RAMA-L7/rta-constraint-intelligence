Conflicts shown as constraint-to-constraint

Duplicates, overrides, contradictory windows and timing-exception overlaps — each presented as two constraints with their source lines, and clearly separated into provable conflicts vs STA-review.

The problem

Two constraints can silently disagree

A later constraint can override an earlier one; two delays can contradict; exceptions can overlap in ways that change intent. Interaction analysis surfaces these with evidence.

Categories

Duplicates

Identical or equivalent constraints defined more than once.

Overrides

One constraint superseding another — shown with both lines.

Contradictions

Provable conflicts with incompatible values on the same object.

STA-review

Overlaps that require path analysis — clearly distinguished.

◈ What interactions do not mean. "Requires STA review" is not a defect verdict — it is an honest boundary: the validator proves structural conflicts but does not resolve path-level winners.
