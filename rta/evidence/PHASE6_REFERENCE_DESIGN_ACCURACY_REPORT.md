## 17. REMAINING LIMITATIONS

- **Constraint_diff (CHG-\*) rules** are not exercised by the reference designs (they need V1/V2 pairs). Covered by unit tests and the diff corpus, but not by realistic reference designs.
- **Heuristic warnings** (SDC-020..045) are policy advisories by design; they cannot be scored for precision/recall and will occasionally fire on legal SDC.
- **General Tcl variable evaluation and cross-constraint conflict detection** remain deferred (Phases 4/5 scope decisions, unchanged).
- Heuristic error rules SDC-005/006 (no input/output delay) fire by design on minimal valid designs; acceptable advisory behavior.
- Severity review (Stage 8): **no misclassifications found.** All verifiable semantic rules are errors; all heuristic rules are warnings; all truly-informational rules are info. SDC-046 as error (vs warning) is justified: a broken `-clock` reference silently alters analysis (the Phase-5 P0).
